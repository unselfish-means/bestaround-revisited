# Best Around (Revisited)

**You're the best around, and now the game knows it.** Best Around (Revisited) plays a sound every
time you level up or earn an achievement. When you die, it plays something more fitting.

## What it does

- **Level up** and **achievement** play *You're the Best* by default.
- **Death** plays *Dumb Ways to Die* by default.
- **Pick your own sounds.** Each event has a checklist of sounds. Check as many as you like and a random
  one plays each time. Leave the list empty to silence that event.
- **No pile-ups.** If the same event fires again within a few seconds, for example dying twice in a void
  zone, the sound plays once instead of overlapping itself.
- **Your volume, your channel.** Sounds play on the Master channel by default, or on SFX or Music if you
  want them to follow that slider.

## Bundled sounds

- *You're the Best*
- Coffin Dance
- Pac-Man death
- Dumb Ways to Die, plus four shorter cuts: short, outro, "so many ways", and "hai hai hai"

## Setting it up

Type `/bar` (or `/bestaround`), or go to **Options › AddOns › BestAround Revisited**. Each event has:

1. An **Enabled** checkbox.
2. A **Sounds** list. Check the ones you want; one is picked at random.
3. A **Test** button to hear it right away.

Settings are saved per profile. Switch, copy, or reset profiles under the **Profiles** page.

## Commands

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/bar` | Open the options |
| `/bar test` | Play the achievement sound |
| `/bar test level` | Play the level-up sound |
| `/bar test death` | Play the death sound |

## Supported clients

One download works on all of these:

- **Retail** (The War Within and Midnight)
- **Mists of Pandaria Classic**
- **Classic Era**
- **WoW: Forever (Classic Plus)**
